# Verified product facts for the Claude guide series

This file is the source of truth for every fact used anywhere in the "Becoming a Claude Power User" episodes: Anthropic plan prices, model names, setting names, menu paths and toggle behaviour, plus the non-Anthropic facts (credit bureaus, IRS, carriers, device makers) Episode 4 relies on. Each fact carries the exact source it was checked against and the date of that check, so a claim in an episode can always be traced back to what was actually on the page.

Re-verify before editing or publishing any episode. Before you change an episode's prose, re-check every row in this file that episode relies on, because the product UI and pricing page change faster than a published guide does. A stale row here is worse than no row at all: it looks authoritative while being wrong.

Source priority when checking an Anthropic fact: support.claude.com articles first, for anything about claude.ai behaviour; then claude.com/pricing, for prices and plan comparisons; then platform.claude.com docs, for model names and API details; then anthropic.com/news, for launch announcements. Facts about a non-Anthropic product or agency (Episode 4) are checked against that organization's own official pages or the governing law. When two sources disagree, prefer whichever was updated more recently.

## Plan and model baseline

Facts Episode 1 depends on most:

- Pro is $20 per month billed monthly, or $17 per month billed annually. (https://claude.com/pricing)
- Max comes in exactly two monthly-billing tiers: Max 5x at $100 per month and Max 20x at $200 per month. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11049741-what-is-the-max-plan)
- Superseded and unverified: Sonnet 5 was the default on Free and Pro and Opus 5 on Max as of mid-2026; the Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 announcements state no claude.ai default. (https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5)
- Last-used model persistence is stated only in the Enterprise default-model article; unverified for consumer plans. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15330088-set-a-default-model-for-your-organization)
- On mobile, the model picker is at the top of the screen; on web and desktop it sits below the text input. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8114491-get-started-with-claude)
- Fable 5.1 is the most advanced model Anthropic makes generally available; its sibling Mythos 5.1 is the same model with different safeguards, offered only through trusted-access programs. (https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1)
- Fable 5 and Fable 5.1 are included on Max up to 50% of the weekly limit, with a separate weekly limit for Fable (row 6). (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15424964-claude-fable-models-on-your-plan)
- On Pro, Fable runs on pay-as-you-go usage credits from the first message; the promotion that included Fable in Pro's plan limits ended July 19, 2026. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15424964-claude-fable-models-on-your-plan)
- Fable is not available on the Free plan. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15424964-claude-fable-models-on-your-plan)
- Usage credits are opt-in: click "Enable" under "Settings > Usage"; they are "billed at standard API rates"; auto-reload is optional. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12429409-manage-usage-credits-for-paid-claude-plans)
- Thinking cannot be turned off on Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.1; on both it is always on at every effort level. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8664678-change-the-model-effort-and-thinking-settings)

3. Exact profile-page field labels (nickname, work function): the personalization article only names "Instructions for Claude."

6. Whether project knowledge files can be replaced in place.
7. Web-search regional availability: no current list.
8. Resolved 2026-10-23: the Usage page has "Weekly limits" with a Fable entry if included. (https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9797557-usage-limit-best-practices)
